Tota Price Manager v2.71 — Persistent Sales/Customer Session

- Sales/customer login now remains active for up to 6 hours across screen changes, reloads, browser/PWA resume and normal navigation.
- Uses Firebase Auth LOCAL persistence; the PIN is never stored for session restoration.
- Session auto-restores only while the persisted Firebase user, access profile and published access remain valid.
- Disabled/expired users are signed out automatically.
- Explicit Log out clears the persistent session immediately.
- Log out & clear offline data additionally removes cached rates, cart, favorites/recent data and cached catalogs.
- Session expiry is capped by the user's access-expiry date.
- Service worker cache bumped to v271.
